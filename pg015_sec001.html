Example 2

Multiply 5 dag 14 dg 15 mg by 8

Steps

1. Arrange the units in columns, with the smallest unit on the right and the largest unit on the left

2. Start multiplying small units to larger units. Multiply 15 mg by 8 to get 120 mg. Remember, 1 dg =100 mg. Thus, 120 mg is equal to 1 dg and 20 mg

3. Write 20 in the mg column

4. Multiply 14 dg by 8 to get 112 dg then add 1 dg to get 113 dg. Remember, 1 dag = 100 dg. Therefore, 113 dg is equal to 1 dag and 13 dg

5. Write 13 in the dg column

6. Multiply 5 dag by 8 to get 40 dag, then add 1 dag which is obtained in step 4; 40 dag + 1 dag = 41 dag

7. Write 41 in the dag column
