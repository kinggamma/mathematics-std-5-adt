Types of fractions

There are three main types of fractions which are proper fractions, improper fractions, and mixed numbers

(a) Proper fractions

In a proper fraction, the numerator is less than the denominator. It means, the value of a proper fraction is always less than 1. The following are examples of proper fractions:

14,24,19,38,1012,1540.
