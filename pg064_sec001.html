Types of fractions

There are three main types of fractions which are proper fractions, improper fractions, and mixed numbers

(a) Proper fractions

In a proper fraction, the numerator is less than the denominator.It means, the value of a proper fraction is always less than 1.The following are examples of proper fractions:

14,24,19,38,1012,1540.

(b) Improper fractions

An improper fraction is the opposite of a proper fraction.In an improper fraction, the numerator is greater than or equal to the denominator.The value of an improper fraction is always 1 or

more.The following are examples of an improper fractions:

65,94,107,119,1211,43.

(c) Mixed numbers

A mixed number is a combination of a whole number and a proper fraction.It is obtained when an improper fraction is simplified.The following are examples of mixed numbers:

312,412,534,1514,2213,1013.

Fractions with different denominators

Fractions with different denominators are fractions whose bottom numbers are different, for example 12 and 13 have different denominators which are 2 and 3.
