Learning more about fraction with different denominator and mixed numbers through online lessons
Descriptions

Use various online resources such as Khan Academy to find video lessons and practical exercises on how to work with fractions that have different denominators and mixed numbers to gain a deeper understanding.

Summary
1.

When adding or subtracting fractions with different denominators, convert them to have a common denominator, then subtract or add.

2.

When adding or subtracting mixed numbers:

(i)

convert the mixed numbers to improper fractions

(ii)

change them to have a common denominator by finding the least common multiple (LCM).

(iii)

subtract or add the fractions with the common denominator.
