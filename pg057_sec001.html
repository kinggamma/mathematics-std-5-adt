Example 1

Write the first five multiples of 2.

Solution

In order to get the first five multiples of 2, multiply 2 by 1, then by 2, 3, 4 and 5 as follows:

2×1=2,
2×2=4,
2×3=6,
2×4=8,
2×5=10.

Therefore, the first five multiples of 2 are 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10.

Example 2

Write the multiples of 5.

Solution

The multiples of 5 are obtained by multiplying 5 by 1, then by 2, 3, 4 and so on as follows:

5×1=5,
5×4=20,
5×2=10,
5×5=25,
5×3=15,
5×6=30.

Therefore, the multiples of 5 are 5, 10, 15, 20, 25

The list of multiples has no end unless a limiting value is given.
