Activity 2
Subtraction of fractions with different denominators
Steps
1.
Use colored paper which is in a circular shape like a pizza cut it into fractions of 12, 13, 14, 16 etc
2.
Subtract 13 from 12.
3.
If 12 and 13 can not be subtracted directly, cut more pieces to make both pizzas into sixths (the least common denominator)
4.
Make the equivalent fractions
(i) show that 12=36 and 13=26
(ii) now subtract 36−26=16.
5.
Practice more subtraction of fraction using fraction cards for:
(i) 14−16
(ii) 23−15
(iii) 38−14
6.
Why do we need to find common denominators?
