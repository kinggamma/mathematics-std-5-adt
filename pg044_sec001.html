Activity 1
Identifying even numbers from 1 up to 12 by using counters
Steps
1.
List the counting numbers from 1 up to 12.
2.
Use counters to represent the numbers.
3.
Put the counters into groups of two, then circle each group.
4.
In step 3 you observed that, there are numbers with groups of exactly two counters with no remainder.
5.
Write the numbers identified in step 4.

The numbers obtained are called even numbers.

Therefore, even numbers are whole numbers which are divisible by 2 without a remainder.

Example 1

List all even numbers between 17 and 33.

Answer

The even numbers between 17 and 33 are 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30 and 32.

Example 2

List only missing numbers that are even in the following pattern: 574, -, -, -, -, -, -, 581.

Answer

The missing even numbers in the given pattern are 576, 578 and 580.
