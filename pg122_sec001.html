Multiplication in simple equations

The opposite of multiplication is division. Therefore, in solving equations which involve multiplication, you can also apply division.

Example 1

Find the value of n if 4×n=12.

Solution

4×n=12
4n = 12

Divide by 4 on both sides of the equation to get the value of n.

4n4=124
n = 3

Therefore, the value of n is 3.

Example 2

Find the value of k if 34k×13=24.

Solution

Multiply terms on the left-hand side of the equal sign (=). That is, numerator × numerator and denominator × denominator.

34k×13=312k

Thus, 312k=24.

In order to convert the fraction into whole number, multiply both sides by 12:

12×312k=24×12
3k = 288

To make k remain alone in the left hand side, divide both sides of the equation by 3.

3k3=2883
k=96

Therefore, the value of k is 96.
