Multiplication of algebraic terms

Algebraic terms can be multiplied by a number or by another like or unlike terms. In multiplying algebraic terms, multiply the coefficients and then multiply the variables. In multiplying terms by a number, multiply the coefficient by the number and then multiply the variables. If the number is zero, then the product is 0.

Example 1

y×y

Solution

In this expression, y is the only variable involved, and both coefficients are 1. When a variable is multiplied by itself, the product is its square. Therefore,

y×y=y2
