Take the denominator, multiply it by the whole number, then add the numerator.

Alternatively

Steps

1.

Subtract whole numbers: 3−1=2

2.

Subtract fractions: 25−15=15

Combine the total, add the whole number obtained in step 1 and the remaining fraction in step 2: 2+15=215
