Changing percentages into fractions

To convert percentage into fraction, take the percentage value you want to convert and write it as a fraction with denominator of 100. After that simplify the fraction if possible to get the simple form.

Example 1

Change 70% into fraction.

Solution

Remark, 70%=70100

Simplify, 70100=710

Therefore, 70%=710.

Example 2

Change 85% into fraction.

Solution

Remark, 85%=85100

Simplify, 85100=1720

Therefore, 85%=1720.

After changing a percentage into a fraction, you can also change a fraction to a percentage by multiplying the fraction by 100.
