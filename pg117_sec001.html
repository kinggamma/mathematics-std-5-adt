Example 3

Rehema is 5 years older than Kemi. If the sum of their ages is 25 years. Find their ages.

Solution

Form an equation by choosing any variable. Let x be the age of Kemi. Note that; Rehema is 5 years older than Kemi. Thus, add 5 to Kemi’s age to get Rehema’s age.

That is, Rehema’s age is x+5.So the simple equation is, (x+5)+x=25.

Collect the terms with variable, then add them.

x+x+5=25
2x+5=25

Subtract 5 from both sides of the equation.

2x+5-5=25-5
2x+0=20
2x=20

Divide both sides by 2 to get the value of x.

2x2=202
x=10

Thus, Rehema’s age is 5+10=15.Therefore, Kemi’s age is 10 years and Rehema’s age is 15 years.
