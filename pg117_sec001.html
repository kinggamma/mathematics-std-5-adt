Example 3

Rehema is 5 years older than Kemi. If the sum of their ages is 25 years, find their ages.

Solution

Form an equation by choosing any variable. Let x be the age of Kemi.

Rehema is 5 years older, so her age is x + 5.

The simple equation is (x + 5) + x = 25.

Collect the terms with variable, then add them.

x + x + 5 = 25

2x + 5 = 25

Subtract 5 from both sides: 2x = 20.

Divide both sides by 2:

2x2=202

x = 10

Therefore, Kemi is 10 years old and Rehema is 15 years old.
