Example 1

Multiply 23 L 200 mL by 7

Steps

1. Arrange the units in columns with the smallest unit on the right and the largest unit on the left

2. Consider the relationship between units of volume; 1 L =1000 mL

3. Multiply 200 mL by 7 to get 1,400 mL. Remember, 1 L =1000 mL. Therefore, 1400 mL is equal to 1 L and 400 mL. 4. Write 400 in the mL column. 5. Multiply 23 L by 7 to get 161 L, then add 161 litres and 1 litre, which is obtained in step 3; 161 L + 1 L = 162 L. 6. Write 162 in the L column.
