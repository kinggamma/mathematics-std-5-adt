Example 1

A carpenter made a table with a length of 3 m 30 cm and 5 mm. What is the total length of 12 tables of the same size in metres, centimetres and millimetres?

Steps

1. Arrange the metric units in columns with the smallest unit on the right and the largest unit on the left

2. Start multiplying the column of 5 mm by 12 to get 60 mm. Remember, 1 cm = 10 mm. Thus, 60 mm is equal to 6 cm

3. Write 0 in the mm column

4. Multiply the centimetres column: 30 cm × 12 = 360 cm

5. Add 360 cm to 6 cm obtained from the mm column. The total is 366 cm. Remember, 1 m = 100 cm. Thus, 366 cm is equal to 3 m and 66 cm

6. Write 66 in the cm column
