Converting decimals into fractions

To convert decimals into fraction, consider the number of decimal places on a given number. Thus, if the number has one decimal place, its fraction will have a denominator of 10. If the number has two decimal places, its fraction will have a denominator of 100, and if the number has three decimal places, its fraction will have a denominator of 1,000. The numerator will be a whole number after converting the decimal into fraction with a denominator of 10, 100 or 1,000.

Example 1

Convert each of the following decimals into fractions:

(a) 0.2
(b) 0.32

Solution

(a) 0.2

Write the decimal as a fraction:

0.2=0.21

Multiply both the numerator and the denominator by 10 to remove the decimal.

0.2×101×10=210=15

Therefore, 0.2 in fraction is 15.

(b) 0.32

Write the decimal as a fraction, that is, 0.321. Multiply both the numerator and the denominator by 100 to remove the decimals.

0.321=0.32×1001×100=32100

Simplify 32100 to get 825.

Therefore, 0.32 in fraction is 825.
