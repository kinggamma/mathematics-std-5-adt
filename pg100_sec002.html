Changing percentages into decimals

In order to convert a percentage into decimal, first convert it into a fraction with 100 as the denominator.Then, divide the given number by its denominator to obtain the required decimal.

Example 1

Change 45% into decimals.

Solution

Write 45% as a fraction

45%=45100

Divide to get the decimal

45÷100=0.45

Therefore, 45% = 0.45
