Example 2

Multiply 45 L 612 mL by 9.

Steps

1. Arrange the units in columns, with the smallest unit on the right and the largest unit on the left.

2. Multiply 612 mL by 9 to get 5508 mL. Remember, 1 L =1000 mL. Therefore, 5508 mL is equal to 5 L and 508 mL.

3. Write 508 in the mL column.

4. Multiply 45 L by 9 to get 405 L. Add 405 L and 5 L which is obtained in step 3; 405 L + 5 L = 410 L.

5. Write 410 in the L column.
