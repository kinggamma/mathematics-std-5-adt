Example 2

Multiply 3 km 604 m 54 cm by 4

Steps

1. Arrange the units in columns, with the smallest unit on the right and the largest unit on the left

2. Multiply from right to left or from the smallest to the largest unit

3. Multiply by considering the relationship between units of length, for example: 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm

4. Multiply 54 cm by 4 to get 216 cm. Remember, 1 m = 100 cm; therefore, 216 cm is equal to 2 m and 16 cm

5. Write 16 in the cm column

6. Multiply 604 m by 4 to get 2416 m. Add 2 m that remained in step 4 to 2416 m. That is, 2416 m + 2 m = 2418 m

7. Remember, 1 km =1000 m ; therefore, 2418 m is equal to 2 km and 418 m

8. Write 418 in the m column

9. Multiply 3 km by 4 to get 12 km, then add 2 km that remained in step 7 to 12 km:

12 km+2 km=14 km

10. Write 14 in the km column
