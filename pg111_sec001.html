Division of algebraic terms

In dividing algebraic terms, divide the coefficients and the like variables.

Example 1

Simplify 4a ÷ 2a

Solution

Identify the coefficients and the variables and then simplify.

4a ÷ 2a = (4 ÷ 2) × (a ÷ a).

Divide coefficient by coefficient: 4 ÷ 2 = 2

Then, divide variable by variable: a ÷ a = 1

Multiply the answer of coefficient and variable = 2 × 1 = 2

Therefore, 4a ÷ 2a = 2.

Example 2

Simplify 4b ÷ 4b

Solution

Identify the coefficients and the variables:

4b ÷ 4b = (4 ÷ 4) × (b ÷ b)

Divide the coefficients 4 ÷ 4 = 1. Then, divide the variables: b ÷ b = 1.

Therefore, 4b ÷ 4b = 1.
