Descriptions

Use various online programs such as Khan Academy to get video lessons and practical exercises about the area and circumference of a circle.

Summary
1.

The value of π is 3.141593…, which is approximated to 3.14 to 2 decimal places, and in fraction, π can also be approximated as 227.

2.

The circumference of a circle with a diameter d is given by:

Circumference=πd.
3.

The circumference of a circle with a radius r is given by:

Circumference=2πr.
4.

The circumference of a semi-circle with a diameter d is given by:

πd2+d.
