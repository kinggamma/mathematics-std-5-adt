Example 2

Convert the following decimals into fractions:

(a) 0.125
(a) 10.06
Solution
(a) 0.125

Write 0.125 as a fraction, that is, 0.1251.

Multiply the numerator and the denominator by 1000. That is,

0.1251=0.125×10001×1000=1251000=18

Therefore, 0.125 in fraction is 1/8.

(b) 10.06

Write 10.06 as a fraction, that is, 10.06/1.

Multiply both the numerator and the denominator by 100 to remove the decimals.

That is,

10.061=10.06×1001×100
=1006100=50350

In mixed number 503/50 is equal to 10 3/50.

Therefore, 10.06 in fraction is 503/50.
