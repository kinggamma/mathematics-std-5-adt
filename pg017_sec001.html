Measurements of volume

When multiplying metric units of volume, start multiplying the smallest measurement to the largest measurement considering the relationship between metric units of volume from millilitres to litres.

Activity 3
Measuring the volume of water by using bottles

Collect three bottles whose capacities are 500 mL, 1000 mL and 1500 mL.

Steps
1.

Use a 500 mL bottle to fill a 1000 mL bottle.

2.

How many 500 mL bottles will be used to fill a 1000 mL bottle?

3.

Use 500 mL bottle to fill a 1500 mL bottle.

4.

How many 500 mL bottles will be used to fill 1500 mL bottle.

5.

If you have 10 bottles with 1.5 L and 500 mL capacities, what is their total volume? Give your answer in litres and millilitres.
