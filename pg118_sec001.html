Example 4

The age of Furaha is 12 the age of her aunt.If the sum of their ages is 54 years, find their ages.

Solution

Let x be the age of Furaha’s aunt.

The age of Furaha is 12x

But the sum of their ages is 54 years

Thus,

x+12x=54
2x+x2=54
3x2=54

Multiply both sides of the equation by 2 to get:

3x2×2=54×2
3x=108

Divide by 3 on both sides:

3x3=1083
x=36

Thus, the age of Furaha’s aunt is 36 years. The age of Furaha is given by:

12x=12×36=18

Therefore, the age of Furaha is 18 years.
