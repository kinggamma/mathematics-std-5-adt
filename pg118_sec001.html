Example 4

The age of Furaha is one half the age of her aunt. If the sum of their ages is 54 years, find their ages.

Solution

Let x be the age of Furaha’s aunt. Furaha’s age is ½x.

x + ½x = 54

(2x + x) / 2 = 54

3x / 2 = 54

Multiply both sides by 2: 3x = 108.

Divide both sides by 3: x = 36.

Thus, Furaha’s aunt is 36 years old and Furaha is ½ × 36 = 18 years old.

Therefore, the age of Furaha is 18 years.
