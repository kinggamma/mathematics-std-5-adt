Example 2

Ali bought a bus ticket for 450 shillings to travel to school and remained with 1,750 shillings. How much money did he have?

Solution

Let y be the total amount of money Ali had before buying a ticket. Therefore, the equation will be:

y - 450 = 1750
Add 450 on both sides of the equation:
y - 450 + 450 = 1750 + 450
y + 0 = 2200
y = 2200
Therefore, Ali had 2,200 shillings.
Example 3

Find the value of v in the equation, v−19=14.

Solution

To make v remain alone in the left hand side, add 19 in both sides of the equation:

v - 19 + 19 = 14 + 19
v = 33
Therefore, the value of v is 33.
