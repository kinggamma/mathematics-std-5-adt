Similarly, suppose that, the four families keep goats as follows: the first family has twice as many goats as chicken, the second family has thrice as many goats as chicken, the third family has twice as many goats as chicken and the fourth family has four times as many goats as chicken. The total number of goats owned by the four families can be written as:

2k + 3k + 2k + 4k = 11k.

In this example; 2k, 3k, 4k and 11k are called terms.

In the term 4k, the number 4 is called the coefficient of k and the alphabet k is called the variable. Similarly, in the term 11k, 11 is the coefficient of k and k is the variable. A term may also have two or more variables, such as in the expression 8pq, where 8 is the coefficient of p and q, while p and q are variables

Identify the coefficient and the variable in each of the following terms:

(a) 6a
(b) y
Answer

(a) In the term 6a, 6 is the coefficient and a is the variable.

(b) In the term y, 1 is the coefficient and y is the variable.
