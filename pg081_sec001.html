Introduction

In this chapter, you will learn about multiplying and dividing decimals with not more than three decimal places, converting decimals into fractions and fractions into decimals. Multiplication of decimals

When multiplying decimals, follow the same procedure as used when multiplying whole numbers without considering the decimal point. Count the required number of decimal places from the right to the left of the product obtained. The number of decimal places in the answer is obtained by counting the total number of decimal places of the multiplied decimal numbers.
