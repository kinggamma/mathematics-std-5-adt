Example 1

Letter x represents the number of oranges required to make the two sides of the beam balance equal. How many oranges are represented by x, if they have the same mass.

For the two sides of the beam balance to be equal, four more oranges are required on the left-hand side. Therefore, algebraic expressions that consider equal values on the right and left sides using a variable are called equations.

Example 2
Study the following equations and identify simple equations:
(a) 3x+5=16
(b) 2xy-7=2x+3
(c) x2−16=0
(d) 4(y+5)=3(y-1)
Answers

Equations (a) and (d) are simple equations because each has one variable and its highest exponent is one. Equation (b) is not a simple equation because the first term on the left side, 2xy has two variables x and y. Equation (c) is not a simple equation because the highest exponent of x is 2.
