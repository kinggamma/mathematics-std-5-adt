Measurements of mass

In order to multiply units of mass, you are required to understand the relationship between metric units of mass from milligrams to tonnes.

Activity 2
Measuring of different objects
Steps

1. Use a beam balance to measure various objects from your environment like empty bottle, bottle filled with water, a paper, a pen, exercise book, stones, sand, pupil bag and a box of chalks.

2. Record the mass of the objects in kilograms and grams.

3. Convert the recorded masses in Step 2 into other three units of mass.

4. Among the recorded metric units of mass in Step 3 which metric unit is the largest? Why?
