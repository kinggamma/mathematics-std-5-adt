Word problems involving sales and purchases

Example 1

An orange was bought at 150 shillings and sold at 200 shillings. (a) What profit was generated? (b) What was the percentage profit?

Solution

(a) Profit is the difference between selling price and buying price.

Selling price = shs 200; Buying price = shs 150.

Profit = shs 200 − shs 150 = shs 50.

Therefore, the generated profit was shs 50.

(b) Percentage profit = 50 / 150 = 1 / 3.

1 / 3 × 100 = 33.3%.

Therefore, the profit was 33.3%.
