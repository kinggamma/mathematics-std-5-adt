Word problems involving sales and purchases

Example 1

An orange was bought at 150 shillings and sold at 200 shillings:

(a) what was the profit generated?

(b) what was the percentage profit?

Solution

(a) Profit is the difference between selling price and buying price.

Selling price=shs 200
Buying price=shs 150
Profit=Selling price−Buying price
Profit=shs 200−shs 150=shs 50

Therefore, the generated profit was shs 50.

(b) Percentage profit is obtained by expressing the profit as a fraction of the buying price:

50150=13

Multiply the fraction by 100 to obtain the percentage profit, that is:

13×100=33.3%

Therefore, the profit was 33.3%.
