Invoice

An invoice is a list of items bought or sold with their prices or unit costs. Invoices are used to keep records of requirements, expenditures, sales and profits in a certain period. The symbol @

which denotes “for each” is used when writing invoices. The symbol is usually placed between the quantity of an item and its unit price which means “price for each item”. Therefore, the cost of the item is obtained by multiplying the quantity of items by the unit price.

Example 1

On 3rd December, 2025, Aneth was given shs 23,000 by her mother to buy school requirements as follows:

  • 2 pairs of socks @ shs 1200
  • 10 small exercise books @ shs 600
  • 15 pens @ shs 250
  • 5 pencils @ shs 100 and
  • 4 erasers @ shs 150. Prepare an invoice of quantities and find her balance after expenditures.

Solution

Aneth’s invoice on 3rd December, 2025 was as follows:

ItemsCost (shs)
2 pairs of socks @ shs 1,2002,400
10 small exercise books @ shs 6006,000
15 pens @ shs 2503,750
5 pencils @ shs 100500
4 erasers @ shs 150600
Total13,250

Aneth spent a total of 13,250 shillings. The amount left after expenditure is: shs 23000 – shs 13250 = shs 9,750

Therefore, her balance was 9,750 shillings.
