Addition and subtraction of algebraic terms

Terms in the algebraic expression are said to be alike if they have the same variable, such as 4k, 8k, 5k and 7k. Like terms can be added or substracted to obtain a simpler algebraic term. In adding like terms, you add the coefficients and leave the variable unchanged. Terms which have different variables are called unlike terms. For example; 3a, 4m, 6p and 7w. Unlike terms can not be added or substracted.

Addition and subtraction of operations may be combined to simplify a long algebraic expression.
