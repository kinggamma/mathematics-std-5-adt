Example 5

The selling price of the commodity is 2,100,000. Find its buying price if it is sold at a 20% loss.

Solution

Let x be the buying price.

Loss = 20% × x = 20 / 100 x = 0.2x.

Loss = Buying price − Selling price.

0.2x = x − 2,100,000.

x − 0.2x = 2,100,000.

0.8x = 2,100,000.

x = 2,100,000 / 0.8 = 2,625,000.

Therefore, the buying price is 2,625,000 shillings.
