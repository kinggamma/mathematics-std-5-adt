Example 2

Change 123% into decimal

Solution

Write 123% as a fraction

123%=123100

Divide to get decimal

123100=1.23

Therefore, 123% = 1.23

After changing a percentage to a decimal, you can also go back to a percentage by multiplying the decimal by 100, then insert the percentage symbol (%) in the answer.

For example, 1.23 × 100 = 123%.
