Division of decimals by decimals

Example 1

5.550÷0.111=
Steps

1. Arrange the numbers as a fraction

2. Multiply both the numerator and the denominator by 1000 to remove the decimals

3. Divide 5550 by 111 to get 50

Solution
5.550÷0.111=5.5500.111
5.550×10000.111×1000=5550111
= 50
Therefore, 5.550÷0.111=50
