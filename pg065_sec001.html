(c) Mixed numbers

A mixed number is a combination of a whole number and a proper fraction. It is obtained when an improper fraction is simplified. The following are examples of mixed numbers:

312,412,534,1514,2213,1013.

Fractions with different denominators

Fractions with different denominators are fractions whose bottom numbers are different, for example 12 and 13 have different denominators which are 2 and 3.
