Example 1

Each obtained number is divisible by 1 and itself and is called a prime number.

Example 1

List all prime numbers less than 20.

Answer

The prime numbers which are less than 20 are: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17 and 19.

Example 2

Write all prime numbers between 101 and 120.

Steps
  • 1.

    List all numbers between 101 and 120 which are: 102, 103, 104, 105, 106, 107, 108, 109, 110, 111, 112, 113, 114, 115, 116, 117, 118 and 119.

  • 2.

    Mark all numbers that are divisible by 2 with X. The remaining numbers are 103, 105, 107, 109, 111, 113, 115, 117 and 119.

  • 3.

    Mark all numbers that are divisible by 3 with X. The remaining numbers are 103, 107, 109, 113, 115 and 119.

  • 4.

    Mark all numbers that are divisible by 5 with X. There is no any number divisible by 5.

5. Mark all numbers that are divisible by 7 with X. The remaining unmarked numbers are 103, 107, 109 and 113. Thus, the prime numbers between 101 and 120 are: 103, 107, 109 and 113.

Example 3

Write the prime numbers in the following list: 19, 21, 29, 37, 44, 45, 49, 51, 55, 57, 59.

Answer

The prime numbers are: 19, 29, 37 and 59.
