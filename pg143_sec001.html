Revision exercise
1.

Find the selling price of certain commodities if the buying price was:

(a) 75,000 shillings and sold at a profit of 15%

(b) 36,000 shillings and sold at a profit of 20%

(c) 2,400,000 shillings and sold at a profit of 24%.

2.

Find the buying price of a commodity if the selling price was:

(a) 32,000 shillings and sold at a loss of 20%

(b) 840,000 shillings and sold at a profit of 12%.

3.

Tumaini purchased a car at 7,250,000 shillings and sold it at 9,820,000 shillings:

(a) how much profit did he get?

(b) what was the percentage of the profit?

4.

Anna was given 30,000 shillings on 17th April, 2025.

She bought 4 litres of milk @ 2,000 shillings
3 kilograms of beans @ 2,200 shillings and
2 kilograms of rice @ 1,900 shillings.

Write an invoice of these items.

5.

Issa received a salary of 450,000 shillings on 25th May, 2025 and bought the following items:

3 shirts @ shs 20,000
2 pairs of trousers @ shs 32,400
1 hat @ shs 4,500 and
1 belt @ shs 3,000.

Write an invoice of these items.

6.

On 9th January, 2025, Thomas bought the following items:

1 pair of shoes @ shs 14,000
3 pairs of khanga @ shs 12,000
3 pairs of socks @ shs 1,500
2 tins of shoe polish @ shs 2,000 and
2 airtime vouchers @ shs 1,000.

If he had 90,000 shillings, how much money did he remain with?

7.

On 14th May, 2025 Kabibi was given 20,000 shillings by her sister to buy the following requirements:

2 bathing soaps @ shs 800
3 washing soaps @ shs 250
3 airtime vouchers @ shs 1,000
1 tube of toothpaste @ shs 1 500

(a) write an invoice of these items

(b) find the remaining amount of money.

8.

On 22nd December, 2025 Mwajuma bought the following items:

3 kilograms of flour @ shs 1,200
5 litres of cooking oil @ shs 1,500
10 kilograms of sugar @ shs 2,000 and
3 packets of salt @ shs 500.

(a) write an invoice of these items

(b) if Mwajuma had 50,000 shillings, how much money did she remain with?

9.

Write an invoice of the following purchases done by a farmer on 2nd September, 2025:

20 kg seeds of maize @ shs 2,000
12 kg seeds of beans @ shs 2,000
12 kg seeds of pumpkin @ shs 500
2 pairs of gumboots @ shs 12,000 and
3 hoes @ shs 8,000. How much money was paid for purchases?
10.

On 2nd January, 2025, an entrepreneur sold a variety of fruits as follows:

50 mangoes @ shs 500
10 watermelons @ shs 2,000
20 pineapples @ shs 1,000
3 bunches of bananas @ shs 15,000
70 avocados @ shs 800 and
30 oranges @ shs 200. How much money did the entrepreneur get?
