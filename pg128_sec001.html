Summary

1.

Algebraic expressions are constructed using the terms.

2.

Algebraic terms are made up of coefficients and variables.

3.

Like terms have the same variables.

4.

To add like terms, add the coefficients. The variable remains the same.

5.

To subtract like terms, subtract the coefficients. The variable remains the same.

6.

Unlike terms cannot be combined to obtain a single term, but they may be simplified.

7.

To multiply algebraic terms, multiply the coefficients, as well as the variables.

8.

A simple equation is an arrangement of algebraic terms which require the left side and the right side to be equal.

9.

A simple equation has one variable whose power is 1.

10.

When collecting like terms to the other side of an equal sign, the operation sign changes.

11.

Adding or subtracting the same number on both sides of the simple equation does not change the meaning of the equation.

12.

Multiplying or dividing the same number on both sides of the simple equation does not change the meaning of the equation.
