Multiplication of two decimal places with one decimal place

Example 1

4.52×1.3=
Steps
1.Multiply these numbers as whole numbers without considering their decimal places
2.That is, multiply 452 by 13 then count three decimal places from right to left of the answer
3.Then, count three decimal places, from right to left to obtain 5.876
Solution
452
× 13
452
+ 1356
5876

Therefore, 4.52 × 1.3 = 5.876

Remember, when working with decimals, zeros that apper at the end of a decimal after the last non-zero digit do not change the value of a number.Example; 3.400 is the same as 3.4.
