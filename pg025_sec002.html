Activity 4
Measurements of length, mass and volume through online lessons

Descriptions

Use various programs such as Khan Academy, videos showing real examples that provide lessons and practical exercises about measurements.

Summary

1.

In order to multiply or divide metric units, it is important to know the relationship among the units. Example, 1 km =1000 m, 1 kg =1000 g and 1 L =1000 mL.

2.

When multiplying or dividing metric units, the simplest way is to arrange the units in columns and then multiply or divide by the given number.

3.

When multiplying metric units, start multiplying from the smallest unit on the right to the largest unit on the left.

4.

When dividing metric units, start dividing from the largest unit on the left to the smallest unit on the right.
