Example 2

Write the variable and coefficient in each of the following terms:

(a) 3pq
(b) 5wz
Answer

(a) In the term 3pq, p and q are variables 3 is the coefficient.

(b) In the term 5wz, w and z are variables and 5 is the coefficient of w and z.

Algebraic expressions are constructed using terms such as 4k, 11m and 12w connected with arithmetic operations.

The following are examples of algebraic expressions:

4k+6k, 11m−m, 6m×3, 12w÷2n.
