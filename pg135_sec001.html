Exercise 1

Exercise 1
1.

A gas cooker was bought at 180,000 shillings and sold at 225,000 shillings:

(a) what was the profit?
(b) what was the percentage profit?
2.

If Halima would have sold her cupboard for 1,200,000 shillings, there would be a loss of 240,000 shillings.

Find the price value of her cupboard.
3.

Find the selling price of a book if the buying price is:

(a) 15,000 shillings and it is sold at a profit of 5%
(b) 9,000 shillings and it is sold at a loss of 10%
(c) 2,400 shillings and it is sold at a profit of 25%
(d) 12,000 shillings and it is sold at a loss of 10%
(e) 4,000 shillings and it is sold at a profit of 5%
4.

Find the buying price of commodities if the selling price is:

(a) 16,000 shillings and it is sold at a loss of 20%
(b) 84,000 shillings and it is sold at a profit of 5%
(c) 5,700,000 shillings and it is sold at a loss of 5%
(d) 1,140,000 shillings and it is sold at a loss of 5%
(e) 6,900,000 shillings and it is sold at a profit of 15%
5.

John bought twenty chickens at 100,000 shillings and fifteen ducks at 126,500 shillings.

He sold chickens at a profit of 25% and ducks at a profit of 15%.

Find the total profit obtained.
6.

Asia bought two hundred holiday cards at 3,000 shillings each.

She sold the cards at 3,600 shillings each:

(a) find the profit from the sales of all the cards
(b) what is the percentage profit?
7.

Mshamu bought one hundred pawpaws from a farmer at 750 shillings each.

He sold each pawpaw at 1,000 shillings.

If all pawpaws were sold, what profit did Mshamu get?
8.

Doto was charged 12% of his business capital as a penalty for failure to issue sales receipts to customers.

If his capital was 8,800,000 shillings, what was the amount payed as penalty?
9.

Jane bought an air ticket to Mwanza at a price of 350,000 shillings.

If 14% of the air ticket price was paid as penalty for rescheduling her flight, how much money did she pay as penalty?
10.

A businessman bought some commodities and allocated profit for each commodity as follows:

(a) one bag of beans; buying price of 240,000 shillings and a profit of 20%
(b) one bag of rice; buying price of 165,200 shillings and a profit of 18%
(c) one bag of maize; buying price of 77,000 shillings and a profit of 10%
(d) one bag of cowpeas; buying price 89,600 shillings and a profit of 12%
How much profit did he get after selling all commodities?
