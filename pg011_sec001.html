3. Write 65 in the dm column

4. Multiply 704 dam by 5 to get 3520 dam. Add 3520 dam and 25 dam, which is obtained from step 2; 3520 dam + 25 dam = 3545 dam

5. Remember, 1 km = 100 dam; therefore 3545 dam is equal to 35 km and 45 dam
