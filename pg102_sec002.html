Summary

1. When multiplying decimals, multiply them as whole numbers without considering the decimal points. Thereafter, place a decimal point after counting the decimal places from right to left of the answer. The number of decimal places of the answer is the sum of decimal places of the multiplied decimals.
2. When dividing a decimal by a whole number, write the decimal as a fraction. Remove the decimal points, then divide by using long division method.
3. When dividing decimals by decimals, convert all decimals into whole numbers.
4. When converting fractions into decimals, divide the numerator by the denominator by using the long division method.
