Factor of a number

A factor of a given number is any whole number which divides that number without a remainder. The plural of a factor is factors.

Example 1

Find all factors of 6.

Solution

Divide 6 by 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.

6÷1=6,
6÷4=1 remainder 2,
6÷2=3,
6÷5=1 remainder 1,
6÷3=2,
6÷6=1.

The numbers which divide 6 without remainder are 1, 2, 3 and 6. Therefore, the factors of 6 are 1, 2, 3 and 6.

Example 2

List all factors of 36 which are divisible by 2.

Solution

Find all the factors of 36.

36÷1=36,
36÷9=4,
36÷2=18,
36÷12=3,
36÷3=12,
36÷18=2,
36÷4=9,
36÷36=1.
36÷6=6,

The factors of 36 are: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18 and 36. The factors of 36 which are divisible by 2 are: 2, 4, 6, 12, 18 and 36.
