Example 3

Given that the circumference of the circle is 88 m, find the area of the circle.

Solution
Circumference=πd
88=22d7
d=28m
Area of the circle=πd24
=227×28×284
=616m2

Therefore, the area of the circle is 616 m2.

Alternatively
Area of the circle=(circumference×circumference)÷π×4
=88×88÷227×4
=88×88×2288
=616m2

Therefore, the area of the circle is 616 m2.
