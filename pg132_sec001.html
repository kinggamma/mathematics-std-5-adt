Example 3

The carpenter made a door at a cost of 250,000 shillings and sold it at a profit of 10%:

(a) what profit did the carpenter get?

(b) how much money did the carpenter get in total?

Solution

(a) The cost of making a door = shs 250,000.

To obtain the profit, calculate 10% of shs 250,000 as follows:

10%=10100

Multiply the percentage profit by the cost of making a door, that is

10100×250000=25,000

Therefore, the carpenter got a profit of 25,000 shillings.

(b) The total amount of money that the carpenter got, is the sum of money for making the door and the profit made:

shs 250000+shs 25000=shs 275,000

Therefore, the carpenter got a total of 275,000 shillings.
