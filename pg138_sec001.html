Example 2

After receiving a salary of 134,000 shillings on 26th April, 2025, Tumaini bought the following items:

6 kg of sugar @ shs 2,400

3 litres of cooking oil @ shs 1,200

10 kg of maize flour @ shs 900

7 kg of rice @ shs 1,600

Tumaini deposited the remaining amount of money in his bank account. Prepare Tumaini’s invoice and then find the amount deposited in the bank account.

Solution

Tumaini’s invoice on 26th April, 2025 was as follows:

Items
Cost (shs)
6 kg of sugar @ shs 2,400
14,400
3 litres of cooking oil @ shs 1,200
3,600
10 kg of maize flour @ shs 900
9,000
7 kg of rice @ shs 1,600
11,200
Total
38,200

Tumaini spent a total of 38,200 shillings

Amount received=shs 134,000
Expenditures=shs 38,200

The amount deposited in the bank

=amount received−expenditures
=shs 134000−shs 38200
=shs 95,800

Therefore, the amount of money deposited in the bank account was shs 95,800.
