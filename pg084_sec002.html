Multiplying decimals by whole numbers

Example 1

Bakari sells 155.25 litres of water daily, how many litres of water will he sell in 20 days?

Solution

Bakari sells 155.25 litres of water every day.

In 20 days he will sell 155.25×20=3,105 litres.

Therefore, Bakari will sell 3,105 litres of water in 20 days.
