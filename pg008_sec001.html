Activity 1

Measuring lengths of various things

Steps

  • 1. Measure and record the length of the top of your desk or table in centimetres.
  • 2. Convert the length you obtained in Step 1 into milimetres and decimetres.
  • 3. Measure and record the length of your Standard Five Mathematics textbook in centimetres.
  • 4. If there are 45 pupils in your class and each pupil has a textbook, what is the total length of the textbooks in metres and centimetres?
  • 5. Use a tape measure to measure the length of your classroom. Record the obtained length in metres.
  • 6. Convert the obtained length in Step 5 into centimetres and millimetres. What will be the total length of 15 classrooms of such kind?
