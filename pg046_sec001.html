Activity 2
Identifying odd numbers from 1 up to 20 by using counters
Steps
1.
List the counting numbers from 1 up to 20.
2.
Represent the numbers by counters.
3.
Put the counters into groups of two, then circle each group.
4.
In step 3, you observed that there is a number with exactly 1 counter and others with groups of two counters and a remainder of 1 counter.
5.
Write the numbers obtained in step 4.

The numbers obtained are called odd numbers.

Example 1

Write all odd numbers from the following list: 38, 49, 87, 96, 105, 254 and 281.

Answer

The odd numbers from the given list are: 49, 87, 105 and 281.

Example 2

Write only missing odd numbers in the following pattern: 1003, 1006, 1009, , , , .

Answer

The missing odd numbers are: 1015 and 1021.
