Example 2

Juma bought a pair of shoes at 25,000 shillings and sold it at 16,000 shillings:

(a) what loss did Juma get?

(b) what was the percentage loss?

Solution

(a) Loss is the difference of buying price and selling price.

Selling price=shs 16,000
Buying price=shs 25,000
Loss=Buying price−Selling price
Loss=shs 25000−shs 16000=shs 9,000

Therefore, Juma got a loss of 9,000 shillings.

(b) Percentage loss is obtained by expressing the loss as a fraction of buying price.

900025000=925

Multiply the fraction representing the loss by 100, to obtain the percentage:

925×100=36%

Therefore, the loss was 36%.
