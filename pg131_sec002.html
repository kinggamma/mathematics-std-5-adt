Example 2

Juma bought a pair of shoes at 25,000 shillings and sold it at 16,000 shillings. (a) What loss did Juma get? (b) What was the percentage loss?

Solution

(a) Loss is the difference between buying price and selling price.

Selling price = shs 16,000; Buying price = shs 25,000.

Loss = shs 25,000 − shs 16,000 = shs 9,000.

Therefore, Juma got a loss of 9,000 shillings.

(b) Percentage loss is obtained by expressing the loss as a fraction of the buying price.

9,000 / 25,000 = 9 / 25.

9 / 25 × 100 = 36%.

Therefore, the loss was 36%.
