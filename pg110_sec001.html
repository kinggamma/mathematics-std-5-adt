Example 3

Simplify 3k×2k

Solution

This expression is a product of two like terms. The variable is k and the coefficients are 3 and 2. Multiply the coefficients and variables: 3k×2k=(3×2)×(k×k)=6k2 (6 is the coefficient and k is the variable). Therefore, 3k×2k=6k2.
