Example 4

Amani bought 40 goats at shs 3,200,000 and sold them at the open market at a loss of 18%. (a) What loss did Amani get in shillings? (b) How much money did he get from the sales?

Solution

(a) To obtain the loss, calculate 18% of shs 3,200,000.

18% = 18 / 100.

18 / 100 × 3,200,000 = 576,000.

Therefore, Amani got a loss of 576,000 shillings.

(b) The amount Amani got is the buying price less the loss.

shs 3,200,000 − shs 576,000 = shs 2,624,000.

Therefore, the amount Amani got after the sale was 2,624,000 shillings.
