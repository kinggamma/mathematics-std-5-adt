Example 4

Amani bought 40 goats at shs 3,200,000 and sold them at the open market at a loss of 18%: (a) what loss did Amani get in shillings? (b) how much money did he get from the sales?

Solution

(a) To obtain the loss, calculate 18% of 3,200,000 shillings as follows:

18%=18100
18100×3200000=576,000

Therefore, Amani got a loss of 576,000 shillings.

(b) The amount of money that Amani got is the difference between the buying price and the loss, that is

shs 3200000− shs 576000= shs 2,624,000

Therefore, the amount of money Amani got after the sale was 2,624,000 shillings.
