Division of decimals by whole numbers

To divide a decimal by a whole number, multiply both the numerator and the denominator by the same multiple of 10 depending on the number of decimal places to eliminate decimals.

Example 1

0.35÷7=

Steps

1. Arrange the two numbers as a fraction

2. Multiply the numerator and the denominator by 100 to remove the decimal places

3. Divide 35 by 700 to get 0.05

Solution

0.35÷7=0.357
0.35×1007×100=35700
= 0.05

Therefore, 0.35 ÷ 7 = 0.05

Alternatively

Steps

1. Divide decimal number as whole number without considering decimal places; 35 by 7 to get 5

2. Separate the decimal places. Count the number of digits after the decimal in the original number, then place the decimal in your answer; 0.05
