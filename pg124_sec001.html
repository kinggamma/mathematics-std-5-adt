Division in simple equations

The opposite of division is multiplication. Therefore, when solving simple equations which involve division you have to multiply both sides of the equation by the denominator.

Example 1

Find the value of x if x÷2=4.

Solution

x÷2=4

Write the equation in fraction to get the numerator and denominator:

x2=4

Multiply both sides of the equation by 2 (the denominator)

2×x2=4×2
x = 8

Therefore, the value of x is 8.
