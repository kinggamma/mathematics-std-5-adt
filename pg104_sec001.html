The concept of algebra

Algebra is a branch of mathematics in which numbers and letters are used. These letters are used to represent numbers or unknown quantities. Algebra uses letters to solve real-life problems and mathematical equations, for example performing calculations when the value of the letter is unknown.

Algebraic expressions

Algebra is used in everyday life to simplify mathematical calculations. For example; 4 families rear chicken and each family has the same number of chicken. If k represent the number of chicken in one family, then, the total number of chickens owned by the four families is:

k + k + k + k = 4k
