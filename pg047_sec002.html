Prime numbers

A prime number is a counting number which is divisible by 1 and itself. One is not a prime number since it has only one factor, that is itself.

Activity 3

Identifying all prime numbers between 1 up to 100

Steps

  • 1. List the counting numbers from 1 up to 100.
  • 2. Encircle number 2, then mark all numbers divisible by 2 with X.
  • 3. Encircle number 3, then mark all numbers divisible by 3 with X.
  • 4. Encircle number 5, then mark all numbers divisible by 5 and have not been marked with X.
