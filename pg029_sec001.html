Circumference and area of a circle

Introduction

In this chapter, you will learn how to find the circumference and area of a circle. Additionally, you will learn how to deduce the concept of Pi. The competencies developed will enable you to deduce and apply the concepts of Pi, circumference and area of a circle in construction, engineering, as well as in tailoring and other daily life applications.

Concept of Pi

Pi is a special number used in calculations involving circles. It is written in symbol as π and it represents the ratio between the circumference of a circle and its diameter.

Activity 1

Finding the approximate value of Pi

Steps

1.
Take any circular object such as a plate, a cup, a bucket or a lid.
2.
Wrap a measuring tape or a piece of rope around the edge of the object.
3.
If using a rope, mark the point where it completes one full round, then measure its length using a ruler. This measurement represents the circumference of the object.
4.
Mark the centre of the circular object and measure the diameter from one side of the circular object to the other side through the centre using a ruler.
5.
Divide the circumference of the circular object in step 3 by its diameter obtained in step 4.
6.
Repeat procedures 1 to 5 using different circular objects with different diameters and tabulate your results as follows:
