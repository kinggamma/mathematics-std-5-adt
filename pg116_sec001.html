Example 2

Find the value of y if, y+12+2y=69.

Solution

Combine the like terms, then add.

y + 2y + 12 = 69
3y + 12 = 69

Subtract 12 from both sides of the equation.

3y + 12 - 12 = 69 - 12
3y + 0 = 57
3y = 57

Divide by 3 both sides of the equation.

3y3=573
y = 19

Therefore, the value of y is 19.
