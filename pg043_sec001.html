Types of numbers

Numbers can be categorized into several types depending on its use. A number can be natural or counting, whole, even, odd, prime and many others. In this section you will learn about the concept of even, odd and prime numbers.

Even numbers

Even numbers are whole numbers which are exactly divisible by 2. The last digit of an even number is either 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. Zero is also an even number.
