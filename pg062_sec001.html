Activity 5
Identifying types of numbers, factors and multiples of numbers through online lessons
Descriptions

Use various online programs such as Khan Academy to get video lessons and practical exercises on types, factors and multiples of numbers to deepen understanding.

Summary
1.Even numbers are whole numbers that are exactly divisible by 2.
2.Odd numbers are counting numbers which are divisible by 2 with a remainder of 1.
3.A prime number is a counting number that has a exactly 2 distinct factors, which are 1 and itself.
4.2 is the only number which is both even and prime.
5.Factors are numbers which divide a given number without a remainder.
6.Multiples are numbers which are divisible by the given number without a remainder.
