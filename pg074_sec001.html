Add fractions with the same denominator

64+94=6+94
=154 or 334

Therefore, 112+94=154 or 334.

Alternatively

Steps

1.

Add whole numbers; 1+0=1

2.

Add fractions, find the LCM of 4 and 2:

12+94=2+94=114
3.

Take 1 obtained in step 1, then add it to the answer obtained in step 2;

1+114=1+234=334

Therefore, 112+94=334.
