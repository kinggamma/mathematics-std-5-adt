Solving simple equations

When solving simple equations, collect terms with variables to the one side of the equation and numbers to the other side of the equation. Adding or subtracting same numbers on both sides of the equation does not change the meaning of the equation. Multiplying or dividing by the same numbers on both sides of the equation does not change the meaning of the equation.

Addition in simple equations

Example 1

Find the value of p in the following equation: p+2=3.

Solution

To make p the subject of the equation Subtract 2 from both sides:

p+2-2=3-2
p=1

Therefore, the value of p is 1.
