Converting fractions into decimals

When converting fractions into decimals, use long division method to divide the numerator by the denominator.

Example 1

Convert 1 20 into decimal.
Convert 120 into decimal.

Solution

This is a simple fraction. Divide numerator which is 1 by the denominator which is 20 by long division as follows:

Steps
Solution
  • 1. Write 1.00 to allow division with decimals
  • 2. Divide the first number: 1 is not sufficient to divide 20, write 0 and a decimal point in the answer
  • 3. Multiply 0 by 20 to get 0. Write 0 below 1
  • 4. Subtract: 1−0=1. Bring down the first 0 after the decimal to get 10
  • 5. Divide 10 by 20, it is not sufficient. Write a 0 after the decimal point in the answer
  • 6. Multiply 0 by 20 to get 0. Write 0 below 10
  • 7. Subtract: 10−0=10. Bring down the second 0 after the decimal to get 100
  • 8. Divide 100 by 20 to get 5 and write 5 in the answer
  • 9. Multiply 5 by 20 to get 100 and subtract 100−100=0
